Using WattTrail

Where do I find WattTrail after opening it?

Click the WattTrail icon or the battery reading in the macOS menu bar to open the monitor. WattTrail runs in the menu bar.

Why are some battery readings missing?

Battery health, temperature, power draw and remaining time depend on your Mac and the readings macOS provides. Macs without an internal battery still show system metrics.

Where can I see past battery use?

Open History · Last 7 days in WattTrail. Battery sessions show observed time on battery, charge levels and average draw when available.
